## 3. Purpose

This policy sets the Organization's requirements for preventing unauthorized physical
access to, damage to and interference with its facilities, Information Assets and
information, and for protecting Information Systems from environmental threats and
utility failures. It establishes Security Zones with graduated controls so that the
strength of physical protection matches the Classification Level of the information and
the Recovery Tier of the systems housed.

## 4. Scope

- **People:** all Workforce Members, Third Parties, contractors, visitors, and delivery and maintenance personnel who enter Organization facilities.
- **Systems:** physical access control systems, CCTV, intruder alarms, environmental monitoring, power, fire suppression, HVAC and water detection systems, and all Information Systems and Media housed in Organization facilities.
- **Data:** all information in physical form (paper, Media, displays) within Organization facilities, and the records generated by physical security systems.
- **Locations:** all owned, leased and shared office space, data centers, server rooms, network and telecommunications rooms, records storage rooms, and loading areas. Colocation and Cloud Service Provider facilities are addressed in section 5.6.
- **Third parties:** Third Parties operating facilities that house Organization Information Systems SHALL meet the requirements of section 5.6 under RSK-003.

Physical security of information and devices outside Organization facilities is governed
by SEC-015. Disposal and Media Sanitization are governed by SEC-017. Logical access is
governed by SEC-003.

## 5. Policy statements

### 5.1 Facility Security Zones

- **SEC-016.5.1.1** The Head of Facilities SHALL classify every area of every Organization facility into one of the following Security Zones and SHALL maintain a zone map for each facility:

| Zone | Name | Typical areas | Minimum entry control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zone 0 | Public | Lobby, reception, public meeting rooms | Staffed reception or controlled entrance during business hours; locked outside them |
| Zone 1 | General | General office space, internal meeting rooms | Individual badge |
| Zone 2 | Restricted | Network and telecommunications rooms, records rooms, HR and finance file storage, security operations rooms, rooms where Restricted information is processed on paper | Individual badge with zone-specific authorization; access logged |
| Zone 3 | High security | Data centers, server rooms, `[PCI]` CDE equipment areas, HSM and key-ceremony rooms, Media vaults | Two-factor physical authentication (badge plus PIN or biometric); anti-passback; access logged and monitored by CCTV |

- **SEC-016.5.1.2** Zone boundaries SHALL be formed by walls extending from true floor to true ceiling (slab to slab) or equivalent barriers for Zone 2 and Zone 3, with no unalarmed openings (e.g. drop ceilings, raised floors or windows) through which entry can be gained.
- **SEC-016.5.1.3** External doors and all Zone 2 and Zone 3 doors SHALL be fitted with door-held-open and forced-door alarms that report to the physical security monitoring station and, for Zone 3, to the SIEM under SEC-012.
- **SEC-016.5.1.4** Zone 3 areas SHALL NOT be identified by signage visible from outside the area that describes their contents.
- **SEC-016.5.1.5** The Head of Facilities SHALL conduct a physical security risk assessment of each facility using the method in RSK-002 at least every 12 months and before occupying a new facility.

### 5.2 Badge and physical access control

- **SEC-016.5.2.1** Every Workforce Member and long-term Third Party SHALL be issued a unique photo identification badge. Badges SHALL be worn visibly at all times within Zone 1, Zone 2 and Zone 3.
- **SEC-016.5.2.2** Physical access SHALL be granted on the basis of Least Privilege and Need to Know. Zone 1 access SHALL be provisioned on HR confirmation of start under PPL-001. Zone 2 and Zone 3 access SHALL be approved by the Asset Owner of the area (for Zone 3, the CIO or delegate) and recorded in the physical access control system.
- **SEC-016.5.2.3** Physical access SHALL be revoked on Separation within the time in SEC-003.7.3, and badges SHALL be returned under PPL-001. Zone 2 and Zone 3 access SHALL be removed within the role-change time in SEC-003.7.5 when a role change removes the need for it (SEC-003.5.7.7).
- **SEC-016.5.2.4** Access rights SHALL be reviewed by the Asset Owner of each area: Zone 3 quarterly; Zone 2 every 6 months. Reviews SHALL be recorded and retained as Evidence under CMP-004.
- **SEC-016.5.2.5** Lost or stolen badges SHALL be reported to the Head of Facilities' security desk immediately and SHALL be deactivated within 1 hour of the report.
- **SEC-016.5.2.6** Tailgating and piggybacking SHALL NOT be permitted. Zone 3 entrances SHALL use mantraps, turnstiles or interlocking doors that admit one person per authentication. Workforce Members SHALL challenge, or report to security, any person in Zone 1, Zone 2 or Zone 3 not displaying a badge.
- **SEC-016.5.2.7** Badges SHALL be configured to deny access outside each holder's authorized zones and, for Zone 3, outside approved hours unless overridden through an approved access request.
- **SEC-016.5.2.8** Physical keys and combination codes for Zone 2 and Zone 3 areas SHALL be inventoried, issued under signature, and reviewed every 6 months. Combinations SHALL be changed within 5 business days of a holder's Separation or role change, and at least every 12 months.
- **SEC-016.5.2.9** Physical access control system logs SHALL be retained for the period set for security logs in CMP-004. `[PCI]` Access to sensitive areas within the CDE SHALL be monitored with individual physical access logs.
- **SEC-016.5.2.10** `[PCI]` Physical access to publicly accessible network jacks SHALL be disabled or restricted, and physical access to wireless access points, gateways, networking and communications hardware, and telecommunication lines within the facility SHALL be restricted.

### 5.3 Visitor management

- **SEC-016.5.3.1** Every visitor SHALL sign in at reception, present government-issued photo identification, and state the name of their host and purpose of visit before entry beyond Zone 0.
- **SEC-016.5.3.2** Visitors SHALL be issued a visibly distinct visitor badge that expires automatically at the end of the visit day, and SHALL surrender it on departure.
- **SEC-016.5.3.3** Visitors SHALL be escorted at all times in Zone 2 and Zone 3 by an authorized Workforce Member. Visitors SHALL be escorted in Zone 1 unless the host has registered them as unescorted for that day and the visitor has signed the Organization's confidentiality terms.
- **SEC-016.5.3.4** Visitor access to Zone 3 SHALL be approved in advance by the CIO or delegate and limited to the stated purpose.
- **SEC-016.5.3.5** The visitor log SHALL record: visitor name; organization represented; the Workforce Member authorizing access; date and time of entry and departure; and zones accessed. `[PCI]` A visitor log SHALL be maintained for the facility and for sensitive areas of the CDE.
- **SEC-016.5.3.6** Visitor logs SHALL be retained for the period set for visitor logs in CMP-004.
- **SEC-016.5.3.7** Recurring maintenance and service personnel SHALL be verified against an approved work order or vendor schedule before entry.

### 5.4 CCTV and monitoring

- **SEC-016.5.4.1** CCTV SHALL cover: all external building entrances; all Zone 2 and Zone 3 entrances; the interior of Zone 3 areas; loading and delivery areas; and reception.
- **SEC-016.5.4.2** CCTV SHALL record continuously or on motion, with time stamps synchronized to the time sources specified in SEC-012, and SHALL record at a resolution that permits identification of individuals at entry points. The Head of Facilities SHALL test camera coverage and recording every month.
- **SEC-016.5.4.3** CCTV recordings SHALL be retained for the period set for CCTV recordings in CMP-004, unless preserved for an investigation or Legal Hold.
- **SEC-016.5.4.4** CCTV equipment and recordings SHALL be protected from tampering and deactivation. Access to recordings SHALL be limited to Workforce Members authorized by the Head of Facilities and logged.
- **SEC-016.5.4.5** `[GDPR]` CCTV SHALL be operated in accordance with CMP-005, including signage informing individuals of monitoring, and SHALL NOT be directed at areas where individuals have a heightened expectation of privacy (e.g. restrooms) or at screens and keyboards.
- **SEC-016.5.4.6** `[PCI]` Video cameras or physical access control mechanisms (or both) SHALL monitor individual physical access to sensitive areas within the CDE, and data collected SHALL be reviewed and correlated with other entries.
- **SEC-016.5.4.7** Intruder alarms SHALL protect every facility outside occupied hours and SHALL be monitored 24×7 by an internal security desk or a monitored alarm service. Alarms SHALL be responded to within 30 minutes.

### 5.5 Data centers, server rooms and network rooms

- **SEC-016.5.5.1** Organization-operated data centers and server rooms SHALL be Zone 3. Network and telecommunications rooms SHALL be at least Zone 2.
- **SEC-016.5.5.2** Racks and cabinets in shared or multi-tenant spaces SHALL be locked, with keys or codes controlled under SEC-016.5.2.8.
- **SEC-016.5.5.3** Food, drink, combustible materials and unrelated storage SHALL NOT be kept in Zone 3 areas.
- **SEC-016.5.5.4** Personal recording devices and removable Media SHALL NOT be brought into Zone 3 areas except with the approval of the CIO or delegate.
- **SEC-016.5.5.5** Work performed in Zone 3 by Third Parties SHALL be supervised by an authorized Workforce Member and recorded in the access log with the associated change record under SEC-013.
- **SEC-016.5.5.6** Equipment SHALL NOT be removed from Zone 2 or Zone 3 areas without a recorded authorization from the Asset Owner and an update to the Asset Inventory under SEC-001.
- **SEC-016.5.5.7** Power and telecommunications cabling carrying data or supporting Information Systems SHALL be protected from interception, interference and damage, including by enclosed conduits, locked patch panels, and separation of power and data cables.
- **SEC-016.5.5.8** `[PCI]` Physical access to CDE system components SHALL be restricted to Workforce Members with a documented business need, and access SHALL be revoked immediately on Separation.

### 5.6 Third-party facilities

- **SEC-016.5.6.1** Colocation, hosting and Cloud Service Provider facilities that house Organization Information Systems SHALL provide independent assurance of physical and environmental controls (e.g. a SOC 2 Type II report covering CC6.4 and A1.2, or an ISO/IEC 27001 certificate covering the facility) at least every 12 months, assessed under RSK-003.
- **SEC-016.5.6.2** Where the Organization has equipment in a colocation facility, the Head of Facilities SHALL maintain the list of Workforce Members authorized at the provider, and SHALL reconcile it with the provider's access list quarterly.

### 5.7 Environmental controls

- **SEC-016.5.7.1** Facilities housing Recovery Tier 0 or Tier 1 systems SHALL meet the following minimum environmental controls; facilities housing only Tier 2 or Tier 3 systems SHALL meet items 1, 3, 4, 5 and 6:
  1. **Power continuity:** uninterruptible power supply (UPS) sized to carry the full IT load for at least 15 minutes, and a standby generator with on-site fuel for at least 24 hours at full load;
  2. **Power redundancy:** dual power feeds to each rack, with power distribution designed so that a single component failure does not interrupt IT load (N+1 at minimum);
  3. **Fire detection:** automatic smoke detection (very early smoke detection apparatus in Zone 3 where available) linked to an alarm monitored 24×7;
  4. **Fire suppression:** a clean-agent gaseous suppression system or pre-action sprinkler system in Zone 3; wet-pipe sprinklers SHALL NOT be the sole suppression over IT equipment; portable extinguishers rated for electrical fires at each Zone 3 entrance;
  5. **HVAC:** temperature maintained between 18 °C and 27 °C and relative humidity between 20% and 80% at server inlets, with N+1 cooling redundancy for Tier 0 and Tier 1 facilities;
  6. **Water detection:** leak detection sensors under raised floors, below and adjacent to cooling units and pipework, and at the lowest point of the room, with alerts monitored 24×7.
- **SEC-016.5.7.2** Environmental monitoring (temperature, humidity, power status, water detection, fire alarms) SHALL generate alerts to the facilities on-call contact and to the CIO's operations team 24×7. Alerts SHALL be acknowledged within 15 minutes.
- **SEC-016.5.7.3** Environmental systems SHALL be maintained and tested as follows, with records retained as Evidence under CMP-004:

| System | Test or maintenance | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| UPS | Inspection and battery test | Every 6 months |
| Generator | Run test under load | Monthly; full-load test every 12 months |
| Fire detection and suppression | Inspection and test by a certified contractor | Every 12 months, or more frequently where required by local fire code |
| HVAC | Preventive maintenance | Every 6 months |
| Water detection | Sensor function test | Every 6 months |
| Emergency lighting and exit signage | Function test | Every 12 months |

- **SEC-016.5.7.4** `[HIPAA]` Repairs and modifications to the physical components of a facility that are related to security (e.g. doors, locks, walls, hardware) SHALL be documented in maintenance records retained under CMP-004.
- **SEC-016.5.7.5** Facility siting and construction decisions for new data centers or server rooms SHALL consider flood plain, seismic, and adjacent-hazard risk, and SHALL be approved by the CIO and the Business Continuity Manager.
- **SEC-016.5.7.6** Facility contingency operations, including access by recovery teams during an emergency, SHALL be addressed in the plans under RES-004 and RES-005.

### 5.8 Delivery and loading areas

- **SEC-016.5.8.1** Delivery and loading areas SHALL be located in Zone 0 or a dedicated area and SHALL NOT provide direct access to Zone 2 or Zone 3.
- **SEC-016.5.8.2** External and internal doors of a loading area SHALL NOT be open at the same time, except when supervised by an authorized Workforce Member.
- **SEC-016.5.8.3** Incoming deliveries SHALL be logged, inspected for evidence of tampering, and matched against an expected order before being moved into Zone 1, Zone 2 or Zone 3. IT equipment SHALL be registered in the Asset Inventory (SEC-001) on receipt.
- **SEC-016.5.8.4** Outgoing equipment and Media SHALL be released only with recorded authorization of the Asset Owner and, where it held data, evidence of Media Sanitization under SEC-017.

### 5.9 Clear desk and clear screen

- **SEC-016.5.9.1** Clear desk obligations of Workforce Members, including the unattended-time limit, are set in GOV-004.5.3.6.
- **SEC-016.5.9.2** Screen-lock obligations of Workforce Members are set in GOV-004.5.3.2; the automatic idle lock is set in SEC-004.
- **SEC-016.5.9.3** Whiteboards, flipcharts and screens in meeting rooms SHALL be cleared of Confidential and Restricted information at the end of each meeting.
- **SEC-016.5.9.4** Printers and multifunction devices used for Confidential or Restricted information SHALL use secure (badge or PIN) print release. Uncollected printouts SHALL be destroyed under SEC-017 at the end of each working day.
- **SEC-016.5.9.5** Confidential and Restricted paper waste SHALL be placed only in locked shredding consoles and destroyed under SEC-017.
- **SEC-016.5.9.6** The Head of Facilities SHALL conduct unannounced clear-desk inspections of each facility against GOV-004.5.3.6 at least every quarter and report results to the CISO.

### 5.10 Equipment siting and protection

- **SEC-016.5.10.1** Equipment processing Confidential or Restricted information SHALL be positioned so that screens cannot be viewed from windows, public areas or Zone 0.
- **SEC-016.5.10.2** IT equipment SHALL NOT be sited directly beneath water pipes, drains or HVAC condensate lines, and SHALL be protected from dust, vibration, electromagnetic interference and direct sunlight in accordance with manufacturer specifications.
- **SEC-016.5.10.3** IT equipment SHALL be maintained in accordance with manufacturer-recommended intervals by authorized personnel. Equipment sent off-site for maintenance SHALL be sanitized under SEC-017 beforehand, or the maintainer SHALL be bound by confidentiality obligations under RSK-003.
- **SEC-016.5.10.4** `[PCI]` Protection of POI Devices from tampering and substitution, including the device list, inspection frequencies and personnel training, is set in SEC-019 section 5.9. The Head of Facilities SHALL provide the physical access and secure storage those requirements depend on.
- **SEC-016.5.10.5** `[HIPAA]` Workstations that access ePHI SHALL be physically positioned and secured to restrict access to authorized Users, as documented in CMP-008.

### 5.11 Physical security of Media within facilities

- **SEC-016.5.11.1** `[PCI]` Media with CHD SHALL be stored in a Zone 2 or Zone 3 area, classified under SEC-002, and inventoried at least every 12 months.
- **SEC-016.5.11.2** Backup Media held on site SHALL be stored in a Zone 3 area or a fire-rated safe, in accordance with SEC-018.

## 9. Exceptions

Exceptions to this document are requested and approved under CMP-003.

The following requirements SHALL NOT receive an Exception: unescorted visitor access to Zone 3 (SEC-016.5.3.3), and `[PCI]` SEC-016.5.5.8 (restriction of physical access to CDE system components).

## 10. Enforcement

Non-compliance is handled under CMP-007.

Lending a badge, deliberately admitting an unauthorized person, or disabling a physical security or environmental control SHALL be reported to the SOC and assessed as a potential Security Incident under RES-002.
