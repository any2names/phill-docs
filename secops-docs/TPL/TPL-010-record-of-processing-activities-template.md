## 3. Purpose

This Template is the format of the Organization's Record of Processing Activities (ROPA). It
captures every field required by GDPR Article 30(1), for processing where the Organization is a
Controller, and Article 30(2), for processing where it is a Processor. It also captures the
additional fields the suite relies on: lawful basis, retention, DPIA status, systems, and the
CPRA disclosures that the Privacy Notice draws from the ROPA.

**Conditional module.** This Template applies only where the Organization processes Personal
Data of EU/EEA residents `[GDPR]` or California consumers `[CPRA]` (README "Conditional
modules"). The Article 30(5) exemption for organizations with fewer than 250 employees is not
relied on, because it does not apply to processing that is not occasional, that is likely to
result in a risk to Data Subjects, or that includes special-category or criminal-offence data.

## 4. Instructions

| Item | Instruction |
|---|---|
| Who completes it | The Data Owner of each processing activity completes Part B (Controller) or Part C (Processor). The DPO completes Part A once for the Organization and reviews every entry. System Owners supply system and security information. |
| Unit of record | One entry per processing activity, i.e. one purpose or closely related set of purposes using the same categories of data and systems (e.g. "payroll", "customer support", "marketing email"). |
| When | Before a new processing activity starts; within 10 business days of a change to any field; and in the review in the next row. |
| Review | The DPO SHALL review the full ROPA with each Data Owner every 12 months and reconcile it to the Asset Inventory (SEC-001) and the Third Party inventory (RSK-003). |
| Format and storage | Kept in writing, including in electronic form (Art. 30(3)), in the privacy compliance repository or privacy management tool controlled by the DPO, with access restricted under SEC-003. Version history SHALL be retained. |
| Availability to regulators | Made available to a Supervisory Authority on request (Art. 30(4)). The DPO SHALL be able to export the current ROPA within 2 business days of a request. |
| Retention | Each superseded entry and each prior version is retained for the period set in CMP-004 row R-18 (privacy accountability records). |
| Links | Retention periods are taken from the purpose-based rule in CMP-004 row R-12; DPIA references from TPL-005; DSR search scope in CMP-010 is taken from field B.11 and C.7. |
| Completion | Answer every field; write "N/A" with a reason where a field does not apply. Tick ☐ boxes by replacing them with ☒. |
