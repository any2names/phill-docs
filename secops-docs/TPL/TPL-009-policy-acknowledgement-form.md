## 3. Purpose

This Template records a Workforce Member's or Third Party individual's acknowledgement of the
suite documents in their Attestation Set, their confidentiality undertaking, and their
information security conflict-of-interest declaration. It provides the Evidence required by
GOV-002 section 5.9, GOV-004 sections 5.11 and 5.12, and PPL-001 sections 5.4 and 5.7.

## 4. Instructions

| Item | Instruction |
|---|---|
| Who completes it | Every Workforce Member; every Third Party individual given an Organization account where the Third Party's contract does not bind its personnel under GOV-002.5.9.7. Section G is completed by the GRC function. |
| When | (1) At hire and (2) periodically, at the times in GOV-002.7.2; (3) after publication of a Material Change to a document in the person's Attestation Set, within the time in GOV-002.7.9; (4) on role change into a new Attestation Set, before the new access is granted; (5) at Separation (sections A, E and F only). Timeframes are set in GOV-002 and PPL-001. |
| Which documents | GOV-004 for everyone, plus the role-based Attestation Set defined by the CISO under GOV-002.5.9.2. The GRC function pre-fills section B with the document identifiers and current versions. |
| Section D | Required for the roles listed in GOV-004.5.12.2 at every annual attestation, and for any Workforce Member at the time a conflict arises. Others complete it only if they have something to declare. |
| Electronic completion | An electronic system MAY be used instead of this form if it captures every field below, authenticates the signer through the Organization's single sign-on with MFA, and timestamps the submission (GOV-002.5.9.3). |
| Where it is stored | Completed forms are stored in the attestation records repository maintained by the Head of GRC, and section D copies are sent to the GC's conflict-of-interest register (GOV-004.5.12.7). Access is limited to the GRC function, HR, the GC and the individual. |
| Retention | Retained under CMP-004 (training records and policy acknowledgements). |
| Questions | Questions about a document's content are directed to its Document Owner; questions about this form to the Head of GRC. |
