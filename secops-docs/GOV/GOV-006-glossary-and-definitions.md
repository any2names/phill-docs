## 3. Purpose

This document is the single authoritative source of defined terms and acronyms for the
Organization's Information Security & GRC Policy Suite. It ensures every document in the
suite uses each term with one meaning, so requirements are interpreted and audited
consistently.

## 4. Scope

This document applies to every document in the suite (ISP, GOV, RSK, CMP, SEC, RES, PPL,
TPL and REF series), to every Workforce Member and Third Party bound by those documents,
and to any procedure, work instruction, contract schedule or training material that
implements them.

## 5. Policy statements

### 5.1 Use of defined terms

- **GOV-006.5.1.1** Terms defined in section 5.3 SHALL carry the meaning given here wherever they appear, capitalized, in any suite document.
- **GOV-006.5.1.2** No other suite document SHALL define or redefine a term. Documents SHALL reference this document instead.
- **GOV-006.5.1.3** Where a suite document needs a term not defined here, the document owner SHALL submit the term to the Head of GRC before the document is approved. The Head of GRC SHALL add it to this document within 10 business days.
- **GOV-006.5.1.4** Where a definition conflicts with a mandatory definition in an applicable law or regulation, the legal definition SHALL prevail for the purpose of that law. The Head of GRC SHALL record the variance in section 5.3.
- **GOV-006.5.1.5** Undefined words SHALL take their ordinary dictionary meaning.

### 5.2 Normative language

- **GOV-006.5.2.1** The key words SHALL, SHALL NOT, SHOULD, SHOULD NOT and MAY, when in uppercase, SHALL be interpreted as described in RFC 2119 and RFC 8174:
  - **SHALL / SHALL NOT**: an absolute requirement or prohibition. A deviation requires an approved Exception (CMP-003).
  - **SHOULD / SHOULD NOT**: a requirement that may be departed from only where the responsible owner documents the justification. No Exception is required, but the justification is subject to audit.
  - **MAY**: truly optional.
- **GOV-006.5.2.2** Clauses tagged `[PCI]`, `[HIPAA]`, `[SOX]`, `[GDPR]` or `[CPRA]` apply only where the Organization is subject to that regulation, as recorded in the obligations register (CMP-001).
- **GOV-006.5.2.3** Text in the form `[DECISION REQUIRED: … Recommended default: …]` identifies a choice for the Organization. Until the decision is recorded, the recommended default applies.
